Field audit tool

Gateward

Photograph a doorway or entrance. Get a measured, ADA-referenced accessibility assessment back.

01 — Upload photos

Tape a standard 8.5×11in sheet of paper directly onto the door for calibration. 2–3 photos from slightly different angles improve results.

02 — Pipeline

  1. 01 Segmenting photos — SAM 3.1 across door, stairs, ramp, obstruction, handrail, threshold
  2. 02 Calibrating measurement — best-angle photo and reference-paper scale factor
  3. 03 Spatial reasoning — Muse Spark synthesizing photos, detections, and measurement
